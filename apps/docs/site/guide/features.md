# Features

## Core Features

### Thumbnail on the watch page
On a YouTube watch page, the video's thumbnail appears to the left of the channel name. When you move to another video without reloading the page, the thumbnail follows.

### Hover Zoom
Hover over the thumbnail to enlarge it (1.75×). This allows a quick look at the image without opening the full view.

### Thumbnail View
Click the thumbnail, or the image in the popup, to open Thumbnail View in a new tab. It lists every thumbnail YouTube provides for the video (19 file names, in WebP and JPG, from both `i.ytimg.com` and `img.youtube.com`). Pick one to preview it, check its real size, and open the file URL or save the image with a right-click. Each tab keeps its own video, even after a reload.

### Popup
Click the extension icon on a watch page or a Short to see the highest-quality thumbnail of that video. On other pages, the popup explains where it works.

### Best available image
The extension checks which sizes actually exist and skips YouTube's grey "no thumbnail" placeholder, so you get the best real image even for older videos.

### Languages
The popup, Thumbnail View and the on-page tooltip are available in English, Japanese and Korean, following your browser's language.

## Browser differences

The features are the same in Chrome, Firefox and other supported browsers.
