## Features

<CardGrid stagger>
	<Card title="One-Click Display" icon="pencil">
		Click the toolbar icon to instantly display thumbnails of the currently playing video.
	</Card>
	<Card title="All Sizes & Formats" icon="add-document">
		View and download all resolutions (from maxresdefault to low quality) and formats (JPG/WebP) in one place.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Shorts Support" icon="setting">
		Works with both regular YouTube videos and YouTube Shorts.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Page Integration" icon="open-book">
		Thumbnails are directly displayed on YouTube video pages. One click to open the detailed view.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
