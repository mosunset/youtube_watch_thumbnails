# Usage

## Basic Usage

### Display from Toolbar Icon

1. Play a video on YouTube
2. Click the extension icon in your browser toolbar
3. The thumbnail of the currently playing video will appear in a popup

### Display on YouTube Page

Thumbnail images are automatically added to the YouTube video page (next to the channel icon).

- Click the thumbnail image to open the detailed view (Thumbnail View)

## Thumbnail View (Detailed Display)

### View All Sizes & Formats

The "Thumbnail View" tab allows you to comprehensively check all available thumbnails for the video:

#### Resolutions (Sizes)

- **maxresdefault**: Maximum quality (1280x720 or 1920x1080)
- **sddefault**: Standard quality (640x480)
- **hqdefault**: High quality (480x360)
- **mqdefault**: Medium quality (320x180)
- **default**: Low quality (120x90)

#### Formats

- **JPG**: JPEG format
- **WebP**: WebP format

### Downloading Thumbnails

1. Select the desired thumbnail in Thumbnail View
2. Right-click the image
3. Select **Save image as**

Alternatively, you can click the image to open it in a new tab and save it from there.

## YouTube Shorts Support

You can get thumbnails for YouTube Shorts using the same operations as regular videos:

1. Play a Short
2. Click the toolbar icon or click the thumbnail on the page
3. The Short's thumbnail will be displayed

## Use Cases

### For Creators

- Check your own video thumbnails
- Research competitor channel thumbnail designs
- Verify thumbnail display at all resolutions

### For General Users

- Save thumbnails from favorite videos
- Download high-quality thumbnail images
- Get thumbnails in WebP format

::: tip Tip
maxresdefault (maximum quality) is not available for all videos. Some videos may only have lower resolution thumbnails.
:::
